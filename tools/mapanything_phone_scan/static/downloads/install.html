Tap Prepare APK in browser, then Save APK to phone. Keep this page open until Chrome finishes saving the file.

  1. Open this page in your phone’s regular browser. Tap Prepare APK in browser, wait for the file check, then tap Save APK to phone.
  2. After the browser finishes saving the APK, open it from Downloads. Android may ask to allow installation from that browser or file manager.
  3. Open RoomWalk, allow camera access, tap Check phone, and select the matching room camera. Tap Capture to open the viewer. Run the 10-second test before your first walk.

Use Record, walk slowly, and tap Stop & save. Keep RoomWalk open while recording. Upload sends the phone video and IMU with the exact room recording attached; originals remain saved. No separate noise recording or printed calibration board is needed for a normal walk.

Version 0.1.11 keeps the main app in portrait and opens Capture in landscape. Upload continues with the screen off and shows progress in a notification. Once RoomWalk has saved the archive, video and timing validation continue on the server. The fixed LAN address is preserved.

The app checks actual native 8K support on your phone. Timing and camera/IMU calibration remain separate checks.
